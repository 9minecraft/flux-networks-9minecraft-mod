# Flux Networks — Power Machines Without Cables

Flux Networks 9minecraft Mod lets you build a completely wireless energy system to move power between your machines. It removes the need for messy cables or pipes in your base. You just place a few special blocks to send energy instantly across your entire world, even into different dimensions.

Wireless Power: Send Forge Energy (FE) from your generators straight to your machines without placing a single wire. Cross-Dimension Transfer: Move power between the Overworld, the Nether, and the End instantly.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Ollie Lansdell**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
